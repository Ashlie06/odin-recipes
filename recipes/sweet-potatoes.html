Mashed Sweet Potatoes

Description

These mashed sweet potatoes made with boiled sweet potatoes, warm milk, butter, and maple syrup are a deliciously simple holiday or fall side dish the whole family will love. This recipe is easy to make in 40 minutes.

Ingredients

  • Sweet potatoes
  • Milk
  • Butter
  • Maple Syrup

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add sweet potatoes, reduce the heat to medium-low, and simmer until tender, 20 to 30 minutes. Drain and transfer to a bowl.
  3. Mash potatoes with a potato masher until mostly smooth.
  4. Gradually stir in warm milk, a little at a time, until potatoes reach desired consistency.
  5. Stir in butter and maple syrup until smooth and butter is melted.
  6. Serve warm. Enjoy!
